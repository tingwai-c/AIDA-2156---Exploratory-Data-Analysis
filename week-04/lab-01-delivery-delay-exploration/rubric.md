# Integrated Industry Lab 3 rubric — 5%

This is one lab submission. The guided and independent sections are rubric
components, not separate submissions.

| Criterion | Evidence | Points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Data setup and integrity | Correct grain; identifier check retained; missing delay values identified. | 10 |
| Guided numeric evidence | Correct, calculated delay summary; IQR-based review table; values are reproducible. | 15 |
| Guided visualization | Clear, labelled histogram and box plot; figures match the supplied data. | 15 |
| Guided interpretation | Plain-language finding accurately describes centre, shape, unusual values, a practical next step, and a limitation. | 20 |
| Independent analysis choice | Appropriate summary and visual are chosen for the selected variable. | 10 |
| Independent evidence | Output is calculated from the supplied data and is labelled/readable. | 10 |
| Independent interpretation | Finding, practical next step, and limitation are specific and supported by the evidence. | 20 |
| Reproducibility and submission | Work runs from the documented instructions; required outputs, meaningful commits, private-repository push, and one D2L link are present. | 10 |

**Total: 100 points (course weight: 5%)**

Possible IQR outliers require review; they are not automatically errors and are
not automatically removed. Unsupported causal claims do not meet the
interpretation criteria.
